# Taste Profile

A running summary of Jesse's cooking preferences. Recipes live in `recipes/`; nutrition defaults in `reference/nutrition-estimates.md`.

## Everyday staple: salad bowls
- **Salad bowls are a regular meal**, often several times a week. Formula: greens + lean protein + fruit + roasted vegetables + a grain or legume + tahini dressing, with nuts and cheese as options.
- **What recent bowls actually look like (Sept–Oct 2026 log):** a small base of baby spinach, grilled wild sockeye salmon (3–4 oz), roasted sweet potato, steamed broccoli, a little quinoa, berries (blackberries and/or blueberries), lemon maple tahini dressing. Rotating add-ins: red cabbage, cherry tomatoes, pumpkin puree or acorn squash, chickpeas, hard-boiled egg, avocado, walnuts or cashews, pear or apple, hummus.
- **Many small portions of many things** rather than big portions of a few.
- **Roasted vegetables are a love**, especially sweet potatoes and potatoes, with olive oil and pepper.
- **Tahini sauces** are a go-to (lemon maple tahini, hummus, baba ganoush, miso-tahini ramen).

## Core principles
- **Acid is non-negotiable.** Finish dishes with lemon, lime or vinegar, more than seems necessary.
- **Lighter, lower saturated fat, without losing flavor.** No butter or oil where it isn't doing a job. Lighter swaps welcome: Neufchâtel, egg whites, fat-free or plant milks, skimming fat off soups, ground turkey, whole wheat pasta. Olive oil is the fat he does use, generously, mostly on vegetables.
- **Comfortable with plant-based swaps**: Just Egg, vegan mayo, plant-based cheese and ranch, non-dairy milks.
- Full-fat favorites kept as-is: original butter-and-parmesan Alfredo, New England chowder, peach pie and ice cream.
- **Fruit as a cooking ingredient**, especially apple pomace / apple puree, plus fruit in savory dishes.
- **Lots of vegetables**; **nuts for crunch** (pecans, walnuts, cashews, pistachios).
- **From-scratch, not processed.**

## Eating out
- At restaurants he often eats **half or a third** of the portion.

## Specific preferences
- **Eggs:** over medium. **Salmon:** wild sockeye, grilled plain, never raw.
- **Bread:** toasted sourdough, toasted dry (thin slices from a round loaf). Also 21 Whole Grains thin-sliced bread.
- **Hot sauce:** Cholula, about 1 tsp at a time; likes real heat too.
- **Seafood lover:** sockeye most often, clams, crab, mussels, crawfish (Crawfish Creole Pasta is a frequent repeat).
- **Cheese:** tangy and sharp (goat cheese, sharp white cheddar, feta, parmesan).
