## Lemon Maple Tahini Dressing (my everyday bowl dressing)
*From my food log: I build it per bowl, right in the bowl or a small dish.*

Per bowl (most common):
- 3/4 tbsp tahini
- 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1/4 tbsp lemon juice

Range I use: tahini 1/2–1 tbsp, maple 1/8–1/2 tbsp, lemon 1/4–1/2 tbsp. About 96 kcal as written.

Stir together; thin with a few drops of water if it's too stiff. Given how much I value acid, try leaning toward the 1/2 tbsp lemon end.

Batch version (about 6 bowls): 1/4 cup + 1/2 tbsp tahini, 3 tbsp maple, 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice, water to thin. Keeps about a week refrigerated.

## Giada's Polyphenol Pesto (Pesto alla Trapanese)
*Source: Giadzy (Giada De Laurentiis). 8 servings.*
- 1/2 cup toasted slivered almonds
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- 1 cup packed basil leaves
- 2 cans Corbara tomatoes, drained (or 1 pint fresh cherry tomatoes)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 3/4 cup freshly grated Pecorino or Parmigiano
- 1 box pasta such as caserecce (to serve)

1. Pulse almonds and garlic in a food processor until finely chopped.
2. Add basil, tomatoes, salt and pepper flakes; pulse.
3. With the motor running, stream in the oil. Transfer and stir in the cheese.
4. Toss with cooked pasta; top with extra cheese, basil and oil.

Freezes well in ice cube trays. Also good on crostini, grilled proteins or vegetables.

## Italian Dressing
*Source: Five Heart Home, "The BEST Italian Dressing." About 1 cup.*
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil (or neutral oil like avocado)
- 1/4 cup white wine or red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp water
- 1–2 tsp honey
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 3 tbsp freshly grated parmesan
- 3/4 tsp garlic salt
- 3/4 tsp dried parsley
- 3/4 tsp dried basil
- 1/8 tsp dried oregano
- Pinch red pepper flakes
- Black pepper

Whisk or shake the liquids, then add cheese and seasonings and shake again. Adjust honey, vinegar or lemon. Also works as a marinade.

## Red Curry Dressing
- 3/4 cup (180 g) full-fat coconut milk
- 1/3 cup (80 g) creamy peanut butter
- 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 garlic clove

Blend until smooth.

## Easy Homemade Pizza Sauce (No Cook)
*Source: JoyFoodSunshine. Enough for two 14-inch pizzas.*
- 6 oz tomato paste
- 15 oz tomato sauce
- 1–2 tbsp oregano
- 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp garlic salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp sugar

Stir sauce and paste together, then mix in the seasonings.

## Creamy Lime Avocado Dressing
*Source: Brazilian Kitchen Abroad. 4 servings.*
- 1 ripe avocado
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves
- 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
- 1/2 cup water
- Salt and pepper

Blend everything in a food processor until creamy. Thin with more water if wanted (then re-adjust salt, pepper and lime).
