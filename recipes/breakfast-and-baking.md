## Sourdough French Toast
*Source: Marcellina in Cucina. Serves 4.*
- 4 slices dense sourdough, 1 inch thick (day-old is best; use more slices if your loaf is smaller or lighter)
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/4 cup brown sugar (can reduce or skip)
- 3 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter, divided

1. Oven to 195°F with a baking sheet ready (to keep slices warm).
2. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until no streaks remain.
3. Pour into a shallow pan that fits the bread in one layer; soak and turn until absorbed, pressing crusts down.
4. Heat a nonstick pan on medium-low; foam half the butter.
5. Cook 2 slices about 3 min, flip, 2 min more; adjust heat so the center cooks before the outside over-browns.
6. Keep warm in the oven; wipe pan, repeat with remaining butter.
7. Serve with fruit, maple syrup or compote.

## Apple Puree French Toast Bake
- 1 loaf bread (French, brioche or whole wheat), cubed or sliced
- 4–6 eggs
- 1.5–2 cups milk
- 1/2–1 cup apple puree
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla
- Pinch salt
- 1–2 tsp cinnamon
- Optional topping: chopped apples, brown sugar, cinnamon

1. Whisk eggs, milk, puree, sugar, vanilla, salt and cinnamon.
2. Pour over bread in a greased 9x13. Add topping if using.
3. Chill overnight. Bake at 350°F for 40–50 min until golden and set.

## Apple Cranberry Orange Bread
One 9x5 loaf.
- 1 1/2 cups (180 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)
- 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (50 g) packed brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) neutral oil (or cooled melted butter)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) buttermilk (or milk + 1 tsp vinegar)
- 1/2 cup (~100 g) apple pomace, lightly packed
- 2 tsp orange zest
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) orange juice
- 1 cup whole berry cranberry sauce

1. Oven to 350°F; grease or line the pan.
2. Whisk dry ingredients.
3. Whisk sugars, eggs and oil until smooth and slightly thick; whisk in buttermilk, zest, juice and pomace.
4. Fold dry into wet until a few streaks remain; fold in cranberry sauce.

## Delicious Pumpkin Bread
*Source: Allrecipes (Vicki Monte), made with fresh pumpkin. Three 7x3-inch loaves (24 servings).*
- 3 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 cups white sugar
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 cups mashed fresh pumpkin (boiled or roasted; canned works)
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 2/3 cup water
- 4 large eggs
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

1. Oven to 350°F. Grease and flour three 7x3 loaf pans.
2. Stir dry ingredients together. Add pumpkin, oil, water and eggs; beat with a mixer until combined. Fold in walnuts.
3. Divide among pans; bake about 1 hour until a toothpick comes out clean.
4. Cool completely on a rack before slicing.

## Apple Cinnamon Oatmeal Cookies (with Apple Puree)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter (softened) or neutral oil
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup white sugar (optional)
- 1 egg (or flax egg)
- 1/2 cup apple puree (cooked down)
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup finely chopped or grated apple
- 1/2 cup walnuts or raisins (optional)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

1. Oven to 350°F; line 2 sheets.
2. Whisk flour, oats, soda, cinnamon, salt.
3. Beat butter and sugars until fluffy; beat in egg and vanilla. Stir in puree and lemon juice.
4. Add dry to wet; fold in apple and nuts/raisins.
5. Scoop heaping tablespoons 2 inches apart; flatten slightly.
6. Bake 12–16 min until edges set and lightly golden. Cool 10 min on the sheet.
7. Store airtight 2 days, or refrigerate up to 1 week.

## Peach Pie the Old Fashioned Two Crust Way
*Source: Allrecipes. One 9-inch pie, 8 servings.*
- 1 (14–15 oz) package double-crust pie pastry
- 1 egg, beaten
- 5 cups sliced peeled peaches
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg, or to taste
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter

1. Oven to 450°F. Line a 9-inch pie plate with one crust and brush with beaten egg (keeps it from getting soggy).
2. *Remaining steps partly reconstructed (cut off in the PDF; verify against the source):* toss peaches with lemon juice; mix sugar, flour, spices and salt and combine with the peaches.
3. Fill the crust, dot with butter, add the top crust, crimp, cut vents and brush with egg.
4. Bake 10 min at 450°F, then reduce to 350°F for about 30–35 min until golden and bubbling. Cool before slicing.

## Peach Ice Cream (Blender, No Churn)
*Source: Christina's Cucina. Serves 6.*
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1/3 cup sugar (on the less-sweet side)
- 1 1/2 large peaches, cut into chunks
- 1/2 peach, diced small (optional mix-in or garnish)

1. Stir sugar into lemon juice (it won't fully dissolve); pour into the blender.
2. Add cream and milk; blend 20–30 seconds. Add peach chunks; blend a few seconds more.
3. Pour into a metal pan and freeze about 1 hour. Stir in diced peach now if using.
4. Stir every hour, two more times (about 2 hr 45 min total), until it sets like ice cream.

## Low-Saturated-Fat Deep Cocoa
One mug.
- 1 1/2 tbsp unsweetened Dutch-process cocoa
- Sugar to taste
- Pinch salt
- 1–2 tbsp hot water
- 3/4 cup oat milk, soy milk or 1% milk
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- Optional: tiny pinch espresso powder

1. Mix cocoa, sugar and salt.
2. Bloom with hot water into a paste.
3. Heat milk separately; whisk into the cocoa.
4. Add vanilla.

## Rice Pudding (Tasting History)
- Heaping 1/2 cup (100 g) small-grain rice
- 1 quart (1 L) whole milk
- 1 cup (150 g) grated jaggery or 1/2 cup (100 g) sugar
- 5 cardamom pods (or 3/4 tsp ground)
- 10–15 saffron threads

1. Wash and soak rice 20–30 min; drain.
2. Bring milk to a boil over medium, reduce to medium-low, add rice. Boil 15 min, stirring every few minutes.
3. When rice mashes easily, add jaggery or sugar and spices; simmer 5 min.
4. Cool before serving.
