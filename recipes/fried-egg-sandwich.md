# Fried Egg Sandwich

A favorite. Simple, balanced, no unnecessary fat.

## Fried Egg Sandwich

### Ingredients
- 2 slices sourdough bread
- Eggs, fried **over medium** (whites fully set, yolk jammy)
- Fresh spinach
- Tomato slices
- Cholula hot sauce
- Mayo, just enough to mix with the Cholula so it clings to the bread

### Method
1. Toast the sourdough dry (toaster, not in the pan).
2. Fry the eggs over medium.
3. Stir a small amount of mayo into the Cholula, only enough to give it body so it adheres to the bread.
4. Spread the sauce on the toast, then layer spinach, tomato and eggs.

### Notes
- Do NOT toast the bread in the egg pan; it adds fat for no reason.
- Sourdough is the bread: sturdy enough for the yolk, and its tang cuts the richness.
- Cholula is the hot sauce: flavorful rather than just hot.

**Nutrition (my usual version):** 2 thin sourdough slices (140), 2 eggs (144), 1 tsp mayo with Cholula (30), light cooking fat (20–40), spinach and tomato (~10) ≈ 345–365 kcal, 18 g protein. Thick bakery slices push it to about 450–550.
